# Try Ghost Hunt on your Mac

Ghost Hunt is Ghostlight's mixed-reality team game for Meta Quest 3. No headset needed: your Mac runs the game as a
venue's station would, and Chrome plays it in a built-in Quest 3 emulator. You need macOS 15+, Chrome and internet.

## Set up

1. Download **[Ghostlight-Station.dmg](https://github.com/SwissConnection/ghostlight-builds/releases/download/station/Ghostlight-Station.dmg)**,
   drag **Ghostlight Station** to **Applications** and open it. Allow whatever macOS asks.
2. It lives in the menu bar and downloads the game by itself. Once its menu shows **Ghost Hunt** and a version, choose
   **Open console**: the guide's page, which runs the game. Click **Add bot** once or twice for teammates.
3. In **Chrome**, open **https://localhost**. Click **Advanced → Proceed to localhost** at the certificate warning,
   then **Enter XR**.

## Play

In the emulator, click the **play button** (▷) at the top, look around with the mouse, and move with **Shift + W A S D**.
**Esc** gets you out.

Pull the trigger to start, pick your color from the orbs, and light up three practice wisps with your beam. Then
click **Start** on the console: lights out, then three waves of ghosts. Hold your beam on a ghost until it bursts; a
ghost that reaches you takes one of your three lives (your left wrist shows them). Wave 3 ends with the Landlord, who
only takes damage while several beams hold him at once.

The console also pauses, plays again, and sets friendly fire, darkness and how many ghosts come.
**End session** in the station's menu stops it.

If https://localhost doesn't load, choose Open console first: the game only runs during a session.
